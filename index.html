Quantum Architecture & Methodology
01
PBKDF2 Key Hardening
Password strings are stretched through 100,000 PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 iterations with a cryptographically random 128-bit salt to defeat rainbow tables and GPU cracking.
02
AES-256-GCM Cipher
Authenticated Encryption with Associated Data (AEAD) ensures confidentiality and bit-level tamper detection. The 96-bit random IV prevents pattern correlation across runs.
03
3D LSB Matrix Inscription
Bytes are split into 1–3 bit atoms and interwoven across the least significant bits of the Red, Green, and Blue subpixels, leaving alpha untouched for lossless PNG conformity.
04
CTF & Forensics Shield
Under 1-bit modulation, pixel variation is ±1 value out of 255. Statistical steganalysis (Chi-square, RS analysis) registers background noise, defeating visual detection tools.
