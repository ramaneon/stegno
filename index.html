Apogee Architecture & Methodology
01
PBKDF2 Hardening
Keys are derived through 100,000 PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 cycles with a cryptographically random 128-bit salt, neutralizing GPU rainbow tables.
02
AES-256-GCM Cipher
Authenticated Encryption with Associated Data (AEAD) ensures both secrecy and bit-level integrity detection with a 96-bit random IV.
03
Matrix Inscription
Bytes are split into 1–3 bit atoms and interwoven across RGB subpixels, keeping alpha intact for lossless PNG standard conformity.
04
CTF & Forensics Shield
Under 1-bit modulation, pixel alteration is ±1 value out of 255. Statistical steganalysis registers standard camera noise floor.
